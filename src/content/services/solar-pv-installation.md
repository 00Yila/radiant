## Power that does not depend on the grid

Nigerian businesses budget for generators, fuel, and the hours lost when
neither is available. Solar changes that arithmetic — but only when the system
is sized against the load you actually have, rather than a package a supplier
had in stock.

An undersized system disappoints from the first week. An oversized one is money
that could have stayed in the business. Both are common, and both come from
skipping the survey.

## What we handle

- Load assessment and system sizing
- Solar panel supply and installation
- Inverter and charge controller installation
- Battery banks — lithium or tubular, quoted with their real lifespans
- Changeover and protection wiring
- Ongoing maintenance, monitoring, and fault-finding

## How we size a system

1. **Measure the load.** Every appliance you want covered, its power draw, and
   how many hours a day it genuinely runs.
2. **Decide the autonomy.** How long the system must carry you with no grid and
   no sun. This drives battery cost more than anything else.
3. **Size the array and the bank** against those two numbers, then quote the
   result itemised — panels, inverter, batteries, mounting, wiring, labour.

You get the calculation, not just the total. If the honest answer is that solar
does not pay for your situation yet, we will say so.

## What we tell you before you commit

- **What the system will and will not run**, appliance by appliance
- **Expected battery replacement interval**, and what that will cost
- **What happens on consecutive cloudy days**
- **Which parts carry a manufacturer warranty**, and for how long

Solar is sold on optimistic assumptions more often than most technology. We
would rather quote a system you are still happy with in year three.
